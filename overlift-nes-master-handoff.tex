\section{Executive Snapshot}

\callout{The central truth}{Overlift NES is no longer a speculative shell. It has three real playable anchors, a clean-room C11/WebAssembly core, modern four-controller support, a hardened AudioWorklet path, explicit DMA and PPU timing improvements, a three-tier renderer, and excellent measured Kid Icarus core headroom. The immediate engineering risk is branch divergence, not feasibility.}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries\color{ink}}p{1.65in}X}
Current state & Three valuable branches contain non-overlapping advances and must be reconciled before further mapper expansion. \\
Immediate delivery & One canonical private project ZIP containing the unified source and every valid supplied game; one public ZIP containing zero ROMs. \\
Validated anchors & Castlevania III (MMC5), Batman: Return of the Joker (FME-7), and Kid Icarus (MMC1). \\
Performance evidence & Kid Icarus averaged roughly 2.02--2.05 ms per authoritative emulated frame in two matching 54,500-frame runs, providing more than eight times real-time machine-core headroom in the validation environment. \\
Compatibility policy & A recognized header or visible title screen is not a pass. Only documented playable checkpoints, stable audio/video/input, deterministic repeat runs, save verification where relevant, and soak evidence count. \\
Content policy & Commercial backups stay private and outside \code{wwwroot}; public deployment and public source packages contain zero ROMs. \\
\end{tabularx}

\section{Non-Negotiable Project Decisions}

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries}p{1.75in}p{5.55in}}
\toprule
Area & Standing decision \\
\midrule
\endhead
NES machine core & C11. Do not rewrite the NES core in Rust. WebAssembly is the browser boundary. \\
Browser runtime & TypeScript application and worker protocol; no JavaScript call per emulated instruction. \\
Host & .NET 10 MVC with a simple local \code{dotnet run} experience. \\
Build & \code{dotnet run}, \code{dotnet build}, and \code{dotnet publish} must compile current TypeScript and current C/WASM source offline after one-time toolchain setup. \\
Execution & Deterministic machine work stays in a dedicated Web Worker. \\
Audio & AudioWorklet delivery is independent of the visual render loop. Sound defaults to On/armed and resumes on the first valid browser gesture. \\
Rendering & WebGPU primary, WebGL2 GPU fallback, Canvas 2D authentic-output fallback. \\
Controllers & Four simultaneous modern controllers through an emulated NES Four Score; keyboard fallback remains on player one. \\
Compatibility & Generic cartridge-board implementations only. No game-specific hacks disguised as mapper support. \\
Diagnostics & Demand-driven. Detailed streams run only while Diagnostics is open; hidden performance telemetry is shut off. \\
Packaging & Lean source ZIPs; exclude screenshots, caches, \code{node\_modules}, \code{bin}, \code{obj}, and generated build output. \\
Public/private boundary & Private development packages may contain the owner's supplied backups. Public packages and deployment output contain zero ROMs and no \code{PrivateCartridges} directory. \\
Studio & Tile/sprite inspectors, semantic profiles, and remaster authoring remain deferred. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\textbf{Exit gates:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Path traversal, nested executable, malformed-header, and duplicate payloads rejected.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{02B.4C --- Automated mapper census and boot sweep}
Every valid image receives a common initial harness:

\begin{center}
Load $\rightarrow$ reset $\rightarrow$ 60 frames $\rightarrow$ 600 frames $\rightarrow$ deterministic Start $\rightarrow$ 1,800 frames $\rightarrow$ classify
\end{center}

Capture CPU jam, run-state faults, visible color count, framebuffer/state/audio hashes, channel activity, mapper identity, and first evidence of accepted input. A title screen is \emph{not} a playable classification.

\section{Future Compatibility Waves}

\subsection{Wave 1 --- Common discrete mappers}
\textbf{Principal games:} Super Mario Bros./Duck Hunt, Contra, Donkey Kong Classics, Solstice, IronSword, Deadly Towers, Gumshoe.

\textbf{Hardware targets:} NROM, UxROM/UNROM/UOROM, CNROM, AxROM, BNROM, GxROM, Camerica variants, one-screen mirroring, and bus-conflict behavior.

\textbf{Gate:} generic mapper self-tests, native/WASM agreement, and at least one real playable checkpoint per board family without title-specific branches.

\subsection{Wave 2 --- Complete MMC1/SxROM family}
\textbf{Principal games:} Kid Icarus, The Legend of Zelda, Zelda II, Metroid, A Boy and His Blob, TMNT, Dragon Warrior I--IV, Nobunaga's Ambition I--II, Romance of the Three Kingdoms, Final Fantasy I \& II, Dr. Mario.

\textbf{Hardware targets:} SEROM/SHROM, SNROM, SOROM, SUROM, SXROM, MMC1A/MMC1B distinctions, banked PRG-RAM, outer PRG banking, battery persistence, reset/power-cycle retention.

\textbf{Kid Icarus butter gate:} doors, shops, item interactions, death/restart, password flow, vertical progression, fortress rooms, boss route, one-hour deterministic run, and physical browser A/V/controller acceptance.

\subsection{Wave 3 --- MMC3, MMC6, and TQROM}
\textbf{Principal games:} Super Mario Bros. 2, Super Mario Bros. 3, Kirby's Adventure, StarTropics, Pin-Bot, and a runnable Megami Tensei patched image.

\textbf{Hardware targets:} qualified PPU A12 edge tracking, IRQ reload/acknowledge edges, PRG/CHR inversion, MMC6 RAM protection, TQROM CHR-ROM/CHR-RAM selection, status-bar splits, dense bank switching.

\textbf{Gate:} multiple scrolling gameplay checkpoints, stable IRQ splits with no status shake, correct saves, and deterministic 15-minute runs.

\subsection{Wave 4 --- Hostile PPU timing}
\textbf{Principal games:} Battletoads, Marble Madness, Micro Machines, Mike Tyson's Punch-Out!!

\textbf{Targets:} sprite-zero timing, 34th background-tile fetch behavior, mid-scanline CHR switching, rendering enable/disable edges, OAM reads during rendering, diagonal sprite-overflow bug, NMI/PPUDATA race cases, CPU/PPU phase alignment, CHR-RAM streaming.

\subsection{Wave 5 --- Advanced Japanese mappers and expansion audio}
\textbf{Principal games:} Akumajou Densetsu, Mouryou Senki Madara, Lagrange Point, Gimmick!, plus deeper Batman and Castlevania III routes.

\textbf{Targets:} VRC6a/VRC6b wiring, VRC6 pulse/saw, VRC7 six-channel FM, Sunsoft 5B synthesis, MMC5 pulse/PCM conformance, base/expansion mixing, mapper IRQs, per-channel activity and PCM hashes.

\subsection{Wave 6 --- CPU, bus, DMA, and full PPU conformance}
\begin{itemize}
\item Exact DMC conflicts with controller, PPU, and CPU reads.
\item DMC/OAM overlap at all alignments and instruction bus ordering.
\item Interrupt edge behavior and remaining CPU timing fixtures.
\item Complete PPU register-race family, exact sprite overflow, odd-frame timing, mapper-visible PPU address timing.
\item NTSC, PAL, and Dendy timing modes.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Wave 7 --- Saves and deterministic state systems}
Implement battery persistence, versioned save states, recorded input replay, rewind checkpoints, crash-safe IndexedDB/OPFS writes, state migration, and deterministic restore. The same state plus the same input must reproduce the same CPU, PPU, APU, mapper, framebuffer, and PCM hashes.

\subsection{Wave 8 --- Liquid browser presentation}
\begin{itemize}
\item SharedArrayBuffer fast path when cross-origin isolated; transferable-buffer fallback everywhere else.
\item Worker-owned deadlines and bounded catch-up behavior.
\item No steady-state framebuffer allocations, unbounded queues, duplicate high-refresh uploads, or growing input latency.
\item Audio remains independent of visual cadence.
\item Stable 60/90/120/144 Hz and variable-refresh presentation.
\item Tab suspension, AudioContext loss, output-device change, controller reconnect, and GPU-device-loss recovery.
\item Thermal/battery-aware presentation policy that never lowers machine accuracy.
\end{itemize}
